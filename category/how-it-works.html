How it works

Architecture, the data model, privacy, offline behaviour and appearance.

📄️Architecture

A static single-page app over Postgres, with edge functions only where the browser must not hold something. What runs where, and why there is no server of our own.

📄️The data model

How LifeWell stores a life — one table per idea, one store behind every tracker, and the three decisions that shape everything above them.

📄️Privacy and security

What leaves your device and what does not — sign-in without a password, per-row database rules, scrubbed analytics, the contacts permission, identity card pictures, and the claims LifeWell deliberately does not make.

📄️Offline and sync

LifeWell pauses the app when the connection is lost, with an optional way to keep supported quick logs on this device until it returns.

📄️Appearance

One icon and one panel for theme, colour, corner radius, density, text size, typeface, surface style and motion. Applied before the first paint.

📄️Language and units

LifeWell offers ten languages across its app, docs and browser extension. Your unit choice stays separate from your language.
