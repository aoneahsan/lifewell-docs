The seven areas

  • The seven areas

    LifeWell is organised into seven areas around You — people first, then memories, everyday and sharing, with health, mind and life stages around them. Six can be switched off without deleting anything; Sharing cannot.

    • concepts
    • domains

  • People

    Where LifeWell starts — you, the people who matter, and what you know about them. Family and work trees, connections, places, messages, community, professionals and blood donation.

    • domains
    • people

  • Memories

    Photos, albums, a scrapbook and the captions that make them readable — with the files themselves in your own Google Drive.

    • domains
    • memories

  • Everyday

    Calendar, appointments, notes, saved places, reminders and the shopping list — the ordinary week that holds the rest together.

    • domains
    • everyday

  • Sharing

    The only area with no switch. What a link can carry, who it is for, what the other person sees, how to end it, and what never leaves your record.

    • domains
    • sharing

  • Health

    Trackers, medications, food and meals, exercise and conditions — one area around the people in your life, there if you want it, and not the point of LifeWell.

    • domains
    • health

  • Mind

    A journal, guided breathing and sleep sounds, mood, gratitude and desk wellness — kept where you can read them back, and never scored.

    • domains
    • mind

  • Life stages

    Cycle, trying to conceive, ovulation, PCOS, menopause, pregnancy and a child's early years — the six screens one profile answer decides you see.

    • domains
    • stages

Features

  • Features

    The things that cut across more than one area.

  • Calendar and appointments

    One calendar for the ordinary week and the medical one, with a checklist on each appointment for the questions you meant to ask.

    • features
    • everyday

  • Reminders

    What LifeWell may send you, when, and on which device — three master switches, quiet hours, and why the app never asks Android for an exact alarm.

    • features
    • everyday

  • Search

    One search across your whole record. The server half is built; the page is not finished yet, and the app says so rather than showing an empty result list.

    • features

  • Community and chats

    Groups with posts and replies, one-to-one messages between connected accounts, blocking, and a moderation route that never tells an author who reported them.

    • features
    • people

  • Professionals

    A directory of practitioners who listed themselves. LifeWell does not check anyone's qualifications — it prints the registration number and links to the register so you can check it yourself.

    • features
    • people

  • Blood donation

    A public directory of donors, requests that reach them, and a contact release that is given once to one person and can be withdrawn.

    • features
    • people

  • The referral programme

    Invite someone, and a confirmed sign-up earns points, a badge and — past a threshold — a month of a paid plan. Every number is an admin setting rather than a constant.

    • features
    • plans

  • The shopping list

    A list built from your meals and recipes or written by hand, and shared as plain text so the person doing the shop needs no account.

    • features
    • everyday
    • health

  • Maps and places

    Your own record plotted where it happened — memories, notes, people and events — plus saved places and an opt-in location history.

    • features

    Which calculators live in LifeWell, which live on ZTools, and why the tools directory is mostly a set of links rather than re-implementations.
