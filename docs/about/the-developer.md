# Who builds LifeWell

**The LifeWell team** builds and maintains LifeWell and this documentation.

The team is direct about what the product cannot do:

- **Questions go to one inbox**, and the LifeWell team reads everything sent there.
- **Not everything is built.** Several things in this documentation say plainly that they are not, and that is
  better than a page describing something that does not exist.
- **There is no schedule.** Where something is planned, this documentation says planned rather than *soon*.

## Contact

[`/contact`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/contact) in the app, or
[`/support`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/support).

## Get involved

[`/get-involved`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/get-involved) is the way in if you want to help — testing,
copy, an eye on the design, or something nobody has thought of.

## This documentation

Written and maintained alongside the app, in a [public repository](https://github.com/aoneahsan/lifewell-docs).
Every page has an edit link at the bottom. If a page here is wrong, that link is the shortest way to say so —
see [Contributing](./contributing.md).

The app itself is closed source.
