# Billing FAQ

## Is LifeWell free?

Yes — all core features are free with no time limit. A premium tier is planned for higher limits + supplementary features. The free tier is the product, not a teaser.

## What's the premium tier?

Premium (when launched) will offer:

- Higher per-memory photo limits.
- Longer audio note durations.
- Unlimited family tree depth.
- Premium themes.
- Full-history insights.
- Priority support.

Free tier covers everyday use; premium is for power users. See [Premium and payments](/docs/profile-and-settings/premium-and-payments) for the latest pricing.

## What are consultation credits?

Credits are LifeWell's prepaid currency for booking 1-on-1 sessions with verified [professionals](/docs/community/consultations) (doctors, midwives, nutritionists, etc.). 1 credit ≈ $0.10 USD equivalent. Sold in packs of 50 / 100 / 250 / 500. Sessions cost 50-300 credits depending on the professional.

## How do I buy credits / premium?

From the relevant page → **Buy / Subscribe** → routes to `https://aoneahsan.com/payment?project-id=lifewell&project-identifier=com.aoneahsan.lifewell` → complete payment externally → LifeWell receives confirmation → balance updates.

The external payment flow avoids the 30% platform fees Apple / Google charge for in-app purchases of digital goods. The trade-off: extra step (open browser); benefit: lower fees → more sustainable platform.

On iOS (once available), in-app purchases via Apple's IAP system will be added alongside the external option (Apple requires IAP for digital goods).

## What payment methods are supported?

- **Credit / debit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover).
- **Digital wallets** (Apple Pay, Google Pay).
- **Region-specific options** (UPI, SEPA, regional cards) depending on the processor's coverage.

## How do refunds work?

- **Premium**: pro-rata refund within first 14 days. After 14 days, no refund on the current month.
- **Credits**: unused credits refundable within 30 days of purchase.
- **Consultations**: per the consultation's cancellation policy (typically 24 hours' notice for full credit refund).

Submit refund requests via [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com) or the in-app **Refund request** form. Most refunds process within 3-5 business days.

## Can I cancel my premium subscription?

Yes — Settings → [Premium and payments](/docs/profile-and-settings/premium-and-payments) → **Cancel subscription**. The subscription stays active through the current billing period; features revert to free at renewal.

## Do credits expire?

No — credits don't expire. Buy 100 today, use them in 2 years.

## Will the premium price increase over time?

Probably not for current subscribers — grandfathering is the typical policy. New subscribers may see different pricing as the product matures. Annual subscribers get a discount over monthly.

## What about students / non-profits?

Discounts available case-by-case for verified students and non-profit organizations. Email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com) with verification (student ID or non-profit registration) for a discount code.

## What about gift / family plans?

Currently no family-plan multi-account discount. Consultation credits can be gifted (you buy, then transfer to a recipient's account — manual process for now via support).

## I see a charge I don't recognise.

Email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com) with the transaction reference + the date. We'll investigate. If fraudulent, we'll refund.

## Where do invoices come from?

The external payment processor (aoneahsan.com/payment) handles billing. Invoices download from there.

## What happens to my credits if I delete my account?

Unused credits: refunded pro-rata if within the 30-day window; otherwise forfeited.

Used credits: paid out to the relevant professional already; not refundable.

## Will LifeWell ever charge for currently-free features?

We won't paywall features that were free at sign-up. New features may launch as premium-only, but existing free features stay free.
