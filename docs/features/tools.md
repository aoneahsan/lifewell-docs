# Tools and calculators

[`/tools`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/tools) is a directory. Most of what it lists is on
[ZTools](https://ztools.zaions.com), a separate site of stateless calculators, and the card takes you there.

That is deliberate. A calculator that already exists somewhere maintained does not get written a second time
here.

## What runs inside LifeWell

LifeWell keeps the health and family records you choose to save. Its child growth chart uses WHO reference data inside a saved child record. The public baby-name browser also stays here.

## What moved out

Four former LifeWell calculator addresses now permanently redirect to ZTools:

| Tool | URL |
|---|---|
| [Waist-to-hip ratio](https://ztools.zaions.com/waist-hip-ratio-calculator) | `waist-hip-ratio-calculator` |
| [Child growth](https://ztools.zaions.com/child-growth-percentile-calculator) | `child-growth-percentile-calculator` |
| [Weight gain](https://ztools.zaions.com/pregnancy-weight-gain-calculator) | `pregnancy-weight-gain-calculator` |
| [Interval timer](https://ztools.zaions.com/workout-timer) | `workout-timer` |

Older calculator URLs still redirect to ZTools. Four old tracker URLs redirect to their saved LifeWell records: `/tools/calorie-counter` → `/health/calories`, `/tools/fasting-tracker` → `/health/fasting`, `/tools/sleep-tracker` → `/health/sleep`, and `/tools/step-counter` → `/health/steps`.

The complete contract is in [Addresses that do not move](../reference/addresses.md#redirects).

## `/health-tools`

[`/health-tools`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/health-tools) indexes health records you can keep in LifeWell.

:::note[The popularity figures are gone]
The 2.x version of this page listed a search-volume figure beside each tool — numbers like *3.2M* that had no
source and were used to order the list. They were invented. They are not in 3.0.0, and the page is ordered by
something real instead.
:::

## Timers inside the app

`/health/workout-timer` and the guided sessions under [Mind](../domains/mind.md#guided-sessions) are part of
the app rather than the tools directory, because they write to your record and the tools do not.
