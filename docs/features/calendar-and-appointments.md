# Calendar and appointments

## The calendar

`/calendar` is a month view over everything dated in your record. It is one calendar rather than a medical
one beside a personal one, because the week does not arrive in two halves.

## Appointments

`/appointments` is the same events, listed rather than gridded, and filtered to the ones that are
appointments.

An appointment carries:

- when and where it is;
- who it is with;
- **a checklist** — the questions you meant to ask, and the results you meant to bring home.

The checklist is the part that is usually written on the back of an envelope and lost between the car park
and the consulting room. It stays with the appointment afterwards, so the answer sits next to the question.

## Reminders

An appointment can raise a reminder. Appointment reminders are on by default and delivered by your device —
see [Reminders](./reminders.md).

## What is in the export

Calendar events export as **Calendar**, and their checklists as **Appointment notes**. Both are in every
format, and the events are the dated kind — narrowing the date range on an export narrows them. See
[the export format](../reference/export-format.md).

## What this is not

There is no connection to Google Calendar, Outlook or any other external calendar. Nothing is read from one
and nothing is written to one. An appointment in LifeWell is in LifeWell.
