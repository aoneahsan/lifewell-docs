# The first run

The first sign-in opens a setup of five steps. Two of them are a sentence each, and none of the answers is
permanent — every one has a home in settings afterwards.

## The five steps

| Step | What it asks | Skippable |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Nothing. It says what the app is for | — |
| About you | A display name, a preferred name, and who you are | Yes, all three |
| What matters | Which of the six areas you want | Yes — leaving it blank keeps all six |
| Reminders | Whether you want a daily nudge, and when | Yes |
| Done | Nothing. It hands you the dashboard | — |

## The one answer that changes what you see

**Who you are** decides whether six screens appear in the menu: cycle, trying to conceive, ovulation, PCOS,
menopause and breastfeeding.

Choosing **Male** puts those parts out of the way. Every other answer — including *Prefer not to say* —
leaves all of them where they are.

*Prefer not to say* is an answer, not a blank. It is stored, it stops the app asking again, and it shows
everything.

Out of the way is not gone. The menu stops listing these, anything you recorded is kept, and any of them
still opens if you go to it directly. Change the answer any time on your profile, and nothing is deleted
either way.

## What matters

The third step is the same list as the [six areas](../domains/overview.md). Turning one off here is exactly
the same switch as the one in settings, and it behaves the same way — quiet, not deleted.

You is not on that list, because You is the account rather than an area you can switch off.

## Afterwards

Everything the setup asked lives in two places once you are through it:

- **Profile** — your names, who you are, your medical basics.
- **Settings** — the six areas, reminders, appearance, and everything about your data.

Nothing in the setup is a one-time decision that cannot be revisited.
