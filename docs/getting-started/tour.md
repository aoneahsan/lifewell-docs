# A short tour

## The dashboard

`/dashboard` is what you land on. It carries the quick actions for the things people record most — a glass of
water, a weight, a mood — so the common case is a tap rather than a navigation.

Logging takes seconds, or it does not happen. That is the whole argument behind the dashboard existing at
all.

## The menu

The left menu is grouped by the [seven areas](../domains/overview.md). It lists only the areas you have on, and
inside an area it lists only the screens that apply to you — see
[the first run](./first-run.md#the-one-answer-that-changes-what-you-see).

## The top bar

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Search | Opens the record search. [Being built](../features/search.md) |
| Notifications | What the app has sent you — reminders, share activity, admin notices |
| Appearance | One icon, one panel, every appearance setting. See [Appearance](../concepts/appearance.md) |
| Your account | Profile, plan, settings, sign out |

## Tracking

`/tracking` is the one screen that shows every number in one place. It is not a separate store from the
individual screens — `/health/water`, the dashboard quick action and `/tracking` are three views over one
table, which is why a glass logged in one appears in the other two immediately.

## Settings

`/settings` holds the things that are about the account rather than about a record:

- **Your data** — export, delete an area, delete the account
- **Domains** — six switches, and a row for Sharing, which has none
- **Reminders** — what the app is allowed to send you
- **Sharing** — who can read what
- **Security** — sessions and sign-out

## Where the honest answers are

Four public pages carry the things people ask before they trust an app with a life:
[`/privacy`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/privacy) ·
[`/terms`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/terms) ·
[`/permissions`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/permissions) ·
[`/delete-account`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/delete-account).

They are written to be read, not to be survived — no defined terms in capital letters, no clause numbering,
no sentence that needs a second reading.
