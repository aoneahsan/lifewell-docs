# Communities

**Communities** in LifeWell are topic-based feeds where users post, comment, and react in support of shared interests — pregnancy support, new-parent questions, weight-loss journeys, mental-health peer support, fitness motivation. Each community has a topic, an admin team, posting rules, and a feed of posts ordered by recency or popularity.

The community module is opt-in. Most users either use one or two communities heavily or skip the module entirely. Communities are the social layer of LifeWell, but they're not the centre of gravity — your health, family, and memory tracking work fully without engaging with any community.

## What a community is

| Aspect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | A defined interest area (e.g. "Pregnancy after 35", "First-time parents", "Type 2 diabetes management") |
| Admin team | One or more LifeWell users moderate posts, set rules, and remove violators |
| Members | Users who have joined; can post and react |
| Rules | Community-specific posting rules + LifeWell's site-wide rules |
| Feed | Posts in chronological or popularity order |
| Categories | High-level grouping for browse (Health, Parenting, Mental health, etc.) |

## Joining a community

From the communities list:

1. Browse by category or search.
2. Tap a community to see its description, rules, and recent posts.
3. Tap **Join**.
4. You can now post and react.

Joining is free. Some communities are public (anyone can see posts without joining); others are members-only (must join to see content). The visibility setting is per-community.

## Posts

Each post has:

- **Author** — your LifeWell profile (display name + photo).
- **Title** — required, the headline.
- **Body** — rich text via TipTap (markdown-style formatting).
- **Media** — optional photos or video.
- **Tags** — community-specific tags for filtering.
- **Reactions** — like, helpful, supportive, etc.
- **Comments** — threaded discussion.

The post format is intentionally close to a forum post (Reddit-style) — substantive titles, longer bodies, threaded comments — rather than a Twitter-style firehose. The pattern encourages depth over reaction.

## Posting

To post:

1. Open the community.
2. Tap **New post**.
3. Choose a flair / tag (e.g. "Question", "Story", "Support request").
4. Write title + body.
5. (Optional) Add photos.
6. Submit.

Posts go through community-specific moderation:

- **Open communities** — posts appear immediately.
- **Moderated communities** — posts await admin approval before appearing.
- **Verified-only communities** — only verified members (those who've completed identity verification) can post.

## Comments and reactions

Comments are threaded — reply directly to a comment, not just the post. The thread is shown in nested view by default; users can switch to flat-time-ordered view.

Reactions:

- **Like** — generic upvote.
- **Helpful** — surfaces posts/comments that contained useful info.
- **Supportive** — for support-themed communities where "like" feels off.
- **Insightful** — for analytical posts.
- **Disagree** — explicit but non-punitive (doesn't down-rank).

No public score totals — engagement counts are visible to mods and the post author but not displayed prominently on the post card. The aim is to reduce performative reaction-chasing.

## Moderation

Each community has 1+ admins responsible for:

- Setting community rules.
- Approving member applications (if private).
- Approving posts (if moderated).
- Removing posts that violate rules.
- Banning members who repeatedly violate.

LifeWell's site-wide rules (no medical-misinformation, no harassment, no hate speech, no commercial spam, no illegal content) apply to all communities. Community-specific rules layer on top.

The [admin module](/docs/admin/communities) covers the platform-admin tools for community oversight.

## Common community types

The platform launches with topical communities covering:

- **Pregnancy** — first trimester, second trimester, third trimester, high-risk, after 35
- **Parenting** — newborn, toddler, school-age, teen, special needs
- **Health conditions** — Type 1 diabetes, Type 2 diabetes, hypertension, PCOS, endometriosis, autoimmune
- **Mental health** — anxiety, depression, postpartum, grief support

Each region's launch may include localised communities. New community proposals can be submitted via [feedback](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com).

## Privacy in communities

Your community profile is **separate** from your private LifeWell data:

- Display name (you can use a pseudonym).
- Profile photo (you can use an avatar instead of your real photo).
- Communities you've joined are public (visible on your community profile) unless you set them to hidden.
- Posts you write are public to the community's audience.
- Comments and reactions are visible to the community.
- Your private LifeWell data (memories, notes, health records) is **never** visible to community members.

The pseudonym option matters for users in sensitive topics (mental health, fertility, conditions with stigma). Sign up with a pseudonym and your real name + face stay private.

## Honest framing — what communities are NOT

- **Not a substitute for medical care.** Peer support is valuable; it's not clinical advice. Any community will repeatedly tell you "see a doctor for X".
- **Not screened for misinformation automatically.** Mod teams flag content, but vetting is human. Take posts as personal experience, not authoritative.
- **Not anonymous.** Even with a pseudonym, your activity is traceable to your account (necessary for moderation). Truly anonymous posting is a different product.
- **Not algorithmically optimised for engagement.** No infinite-scroll engagement loops, no "what's hot" engagement bait. The default sort is recency, not algorithm.
- **Not monetised through ads.** Communities don't carry advertising. Premium support of LifeWell funds the platform, not ad surveillance.

## Frequently asked

**Can I start my own community?**
Yes — submit a community proposal from the communities page. The platform reviews to avoid duplicate communities and to ensure the topic isn't going to require heavy moderation overhead.

**Can I leave a community?**
Yes — open the community → **Leave**. Your posts stay (so the conversation isn't disrupted) but you no longer get notifications and can no longer post.

**Can I delete a post I made?**
Yes — your own posts and comments can be deleted at any time. Deletion is soft (a "post deleted" placeholder remains for thread context); hard delete also available.

**What if I'm being harassed?**
Use the **Report** button on the offending content. The community admin and (for severe cases) platform admins review. Block individual users from the [chats](./chats) settings.

**Can I export my community posts?**
Yes — from your community profile, **Export my posts** generates a JSON archive of all posts and comments you've made.
