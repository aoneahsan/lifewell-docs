# Everyday

The ordinary week. Not the interesting area, and the one most people open most often.

## Calendar and appointments

`/calendar` is the month, `/appointments` is the list. An appointment carries a checklist — the questions you
meant to ask, the results you meant to collect — which is the part that is usually written on the back of an
envelope and lost.

See [Calendar and appointments](../features/calendar-and-appointments.md).

## Notes

`/notes` holds written notes, in folders. Two things about them are worth knowing:

- **Every note is rich text.** There is no plain textarea anywhere in LifeWell.
- **Deleting a folder never deletes its notes.** They come out of the folder and stay in your record. That
  promise is enforced by the database rather than by the screen that asks the question.

`/maps/notes` plots the ones that have a place attached.

## Saved places

A place you want to remember, with whatever you wanted to remember about it. They appear on `/maps` and in
the export.

## Reminders

`/profile/reminders` is where you say what LifeWell may send you and when. Medication and appointment
reminders are on; the nudge for a day you have not written anything in is off until you ask for it. See
[Reminders](../features/reminders.md).

## The shopping list

Built from meals and recipes, or written by hand, and shareable as plain text so the person doing the shop
does not need an account. See [The shopping list](../features/shopping-list.md).

## Why "everyday" and not "memories"

Notes are filed here rather than under Memories, and the distinction is the one the product uses everywhere:
**Memories** is captions, dates, albums and the link to each file. **Everyday** is calendar, notes and saved
places. A note about a hospital appointment is not a memory, however much it is a record.
