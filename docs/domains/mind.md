# Mind

The smallest area by screen count and the one with the strictest rules about what leaves it.

## The journal

`/health/mood-journal` is the way in. A journal entry is whatever you noticed. This is a note to yourself,
not a theory — nothing on this page will turn it into one.

The journal covers several kinds of entry: mood, gratitude, dreams, and free writing. They are one store with
a kind on each row, which is why a month reads as a month rather than as four separate months.

:::note[The journal is never in the PDF export]
A PDF is a document people print, hand to a receptionist and leave on a desk. The journal is the one thing in
LifeWell that must not leave that way. It is in the JSON and CSV exports, which are files you open yourself.
See [the export format](../reference/export-format.md#what-each-format-leaves-out).
:::

## Guided sessions

`/health/breathing`, `/health/meditation`, `/health/sleep-sounds`, `/health/affirmations`,
`/health/panic-attack` and `/health/stress-assessment`.

Sessions are only recorded when you ask for them to be. An empty list here means the page has been doing its
job quietly.

## Desk wellness

`/wellness` holds the short interruptions for a working day — an eye break, a movement break, a posture
prompt, a glass of water, a medication nudge. Each can be turned on independently and each is off by default.

A hundred breaks across fourteen days is not a streak. There is nothing to break, and a quiet Sunday is just
a quiet Sunday.

## What is deliberately absent

No mood score. No wellness index. No weekly report telling you how you did. LifeWell holds what you wrote and
shows it back to you in a shape you can read; it does not have an opinion about it.

If a number would be useful to you, the trackers in [Health](./health.md) will hold one — that is your choice
to make rather than the app's.
