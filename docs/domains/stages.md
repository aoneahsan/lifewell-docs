# Life stages

The area that covers the parts of a life that are not the same every year.

## The six that depend on who you are

One answer on your profile decides whether these are listed in the menu:

| Screen | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Cycle | Predictions that adjust to you rather than to an average of other people |
| Trying to conceive | Windows, tests and notes, in one place instead of three apps and a notebook |
| Ovulation | The likely fertile days in a cycle, with the caveat that cycles vary |
| PCOS | A condition record like any other — symptoms, appointments, and what a clinic said |
| Menopause | Symptoms and how they move over months, kept where you can read them back |
| Breastfeeding | Feeds and their times, inside a baby's own record |

Choosing **Male** puts those parts out of the way. Every other answer leaves all of them where they are, and
nothing is deleted either way.

Out of the way is not gone: the menu stops listing them, anything you recorded is kept, and each still opens
if you go to it directly.

## Cycle

`/period` records days and predicts the next one from **your** history rather than from a 28-day assumption.
Predictions carry their own caveat on the page: a cycle that varies produces a prediction that varies, and the
app says so instead of presenting a date as a fact.

## Baby

`/baby` holds a child's early record, and it is per child rather than one merged list:

- **Feeds** — `/baby/feeding`
- **Sleep** — `/baby/sleep`
- **Nappies** — `/baby/diapers`
- **Growth** — `/baby/growth`, plotted rather than tabulated
- **Milestones** — `/baby/milestones`

`/tools/child-growth` is the public calculator behind the growth chart, and it works without an account.

## Baby names

[`/baby-names`](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/baby-names) is a public page. It needs no account and stores
nothing about you.

## Sharing a stage with someone

A partner or a family member can be given read access to part of this area rather than all of it. That is a
sharing decision rather than a life-stage one — see [Sharing](../features/sharing.md).
