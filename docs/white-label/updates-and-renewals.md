# Updates and renewals

Every licence and every buyout comes with **six months** of product updates from the day you buy.

## What counts as an update

Fixes, improvements and new features that LifeWell itself receives, applied to your copy by us. Changes made only for
you are not updates; they are [custom work](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

## After the six months

Your copy keeps running on its last version. A renewal of three, six or twelve months brings updates back. It starts
when your current period ends, or today if it already has.

Renew on [the updates page](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email address the licence was
issued to. Renewal prices are listed there.
