# White-label questions

## Do we get the source code with a licence?

No. A licence is LifeWell run for you, under your name. The source code changes hands only with a
[buyout](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

## Can you sell LifeWell to someone else as well?

Yes, until somebody buys it outright. A licence is not exclusive. After a buyout we stop selling it, and licences sold
before then keep their terms.

## Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty. The buyer pays us 7% of the gross revenue LifeWell earns, not counting sales tax or VAT collected,
every quarter with a revenue statement. It runs for as long as LifeWell earns revenue, and it is still owed after a
resale. The [licence terms](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms#wlt-buyout) set it out.

## How do we pay?

By bank transfer to our Wise account. The payment page shows the details and asks for the transfer reference. Once we
confirm the transfer, your licence key arrives by email.

## What happens when the six months of updates end?

Your copy keeps running on the version it has. A renewal of three, six or twelve months brings updates back.

## Is there an iPhone app?

LifeWell ships on the web and on Android today. The mobile licence covers both phones; the iOS build is made when you
provide or fund an Apple developer account.

## What do you need from us to start?

Your domain, your name as it should appear, a logo — an SVG is best — and your colours. For Android, access to your
Google Play developer account.

## Can we ask before buying?

Yes — [ask for a quote, custom work or a call](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
