# What is delivered on each platform

A licence covers the platforms you pay for. LifeWell ships two today, and only those two are offered.

## Web app

- LifeWell served from your domain, with the certificate issued and renewed by us.
- Your name, logo and colours on every screen, in the browser tab, on the sign-in page and in the emails it sends.
- Hosting, deployment and every update in the update period.

**What we need:** the domain (or a subdomain of yours), your name as it should appear, a logo — an SVG is best — and
your colours.

## Android + iOS app

- The Android app, built under your name and published on **your own** Google Play developer account.
- The iOS build, made when you provide or fund an Apple developer account. Until then the mobile licence delivers
  Android.

**What we need:** access to your Google Play developer account, and later the Apple account if you want iOS.

## On every licensed copy

- A licence key and a licence file at `/.well-known/lifewell-white-label-license.json`, so anyone can
  [check it](./verify-a-licence.md).
- Six months of updates from the day you buy ([updates and renewals](./updates-and-renewals.md)).

Prices per platform: [the pricing page](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing).
