# White-label and licensing

A white-label licence lets an organisation offer LifeWell to its own people, with its name, its logo and its colours on
every screen. We host it, put it on your domain and keep it updated. The source code stays with us unless you buy it
outright.

Prices, and the form to buy, are on [the white-label pages](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label). This section explains what you get; it
does not repeat the numbers, because the pricing page reads them live.

## Who it suits

An organisation whose people keep a life together: a care home whose families keep a parent's stories, a
family-history society, an employer or a union offering a staff benefit, a community or faith organisation.

## Two ways to buy

| | A licence | A buyout |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | LifeWell run for you, under your name | The source code of the web and Android apps |
| Who runs it | We do | You do |
| Exclusive | No — we may license it to others | Yes — we stop selling LifeWell to anyone else |
| Updates | Six months, then renewals | Six months, then renewals |
| Price | [Per platform](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing) | [Once](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout) |

Changes made only for your copy are [custom work](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted separately.

## What does not change

The people who use your copy keep the rights anyone using LifeWell has: what they enter is theirs, they can export all
of it, and they can delete their account themselves. It is still not a health tracker, a medical device or a clinical
record, and a licensed copy cannot be offered as one.

The licence in plain words: [licence terms](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms). The full agreement: the
[Terms of Service](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/terms#t-whitelabel).
