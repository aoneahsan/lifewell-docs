# Moving from Remento to LifeWell

Remento is about a parent's voice. It sends spoken prompts with no app and no login, turns each recording into a written chapter, and prints a hardcover with a QR code that plays the voice back.

LifeWell is not a book project. It is one free place for the people, their memories, the tree and the appointments.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Every fact here is also on [LifeWell and Remento](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/remento).

## After the chapters are written

1. Download the recordings and photographs from Remento. It lets you take them out, and they are the originals.
2. [Sign in to LifeWell](/getting-started/first-run) and add the parent who told the stories in [People](/domains/people).
3. Make a [memory](/domains/memories) for each chapter you want beside them, with the photographs and the date.
4. For new stories, Speak it records and writes down what is said. The recording stays private to you until you delete it.
5. Keep Remento for the printed book and the QR code that plays the voice.

Remento's home page did not state a record per person, a family tree, share links, appointments, health records, notes or a free plan.

LifeWell prints no book and no tree.
