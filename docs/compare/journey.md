# Moving from Journey to LifeWell

Journey is a diary: a free version you can use indefinitely, and a membership that adds end-to-end encryption, shared journals and export to ePub and DOCX.

LifeWell is about people as much as days. A memory is filed under the people in it, next to the family tree and their health and appointments.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Compare the two on the app: [LifeWell and Journey](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/journey).

## From diary entries to people

1. Export your journal to DOCX or ePub through Journey's membership, so the full text sits in a file you keep.
2. [Sign in to LifeWell](/getting-started/first-run).
3. Add the people your entries are about in [People](/domains/people).
4. Copy across the entries you want as [memories](/domains/memories), with the photos, the date and the place.
5. Keep Journey for the private daily diary.

## Journey and LifeWell

| Feature | LifeWell | Journey |
|---|---|---|
| Memories with a photo, a date and a place | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://journey.cloud/membership" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Share a page with someone who has no account | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://journey.cloud/membership" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Download your own data | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://journey.cloud/membership" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://journey.cloud/membership" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

Journey's membership page did not state a record per person, a family tree, speaking an entry, appointments, health records, notes, an Android app or a household plan.

A Journey web link shows a journal entry. A LifeWell link shows a tree, a tracker or medications instead ([sharing](/domains/sharing)).
