# Moving from Obsidian to LifeWell

Obsidian keeps notes as plain files on your own device, free without limits, with paid sync and publishing.

LifeWell gives you structure you did not have to design. People, a tree, memories, health and appointments come already linked.

Last checked 2026-11-07. See the comparison on [LifeWell and Obsidian](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/obsidian).

## From a vault to a record

1. [Sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. If your vault has a note for each person, paste their names into the import page in [People](/domains/people), one per line. It shows everyone it found before saving.
3. Copy the body of each person's note into their record, or into [notes](/domains/everyday), which are rich text.
4. Dated notes — a check-up, an anniversary — go on the [calendar](/features/calendar-and-appointments) with a [reminder](/features/reminders).
5. Keep the vault. Your Markdown files stay on your device whatever you do in LifeWell.

Obsidian's pricing page did not mention a record per person, a family tree, memories, speaking an entry, appointments, health records, downloading your data or a household plan.

Your LifeWell record lives in its database, not as files on your device. You can download all of it as JSON, CSV or PDF at any time ([export](/your-data/export)).
