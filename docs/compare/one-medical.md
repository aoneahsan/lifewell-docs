# Moving from One Medical to LifeWell

One Medical is the doctor's office itself: a US primary-care membership with in-office and video appointments, and your clinic record in its app.

LifeWell is not care. It is your own record of appointments and health across every clinic, free, kept with the people in your life.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Both products, with sources: [LifeWell and One Medical](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/one-medical).

## Keeping your own record of One Medical visits

1. Keep the membership for care. LifeWell books nothing and does not connect to One Medical.
2. [Sign in to LifeWell](/getting-started/first-run).
3. After each visit, in the office or on video, add it as an [appointment](/features/calendar-and-appointments), with what you asked and what you were told in its checklist.
4. Results and medicines from the visit go into [health](/domains/health).
5. A child's medicines have a list of their own, kept apart from adult doses. LifeWell is not medical advice.
