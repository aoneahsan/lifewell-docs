# Moving from TickTick to LifeWell

TickTick puts tasks, habits and a calendar in one app on every platform, free, with a yearly Premium. LifeWell has no task list.

LifeWell's calendar is for the people in it, and those people carry memories, a tree and health records.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Both, row by row: [LifeWell and TickTick](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/ticktick).

## Using TickTick and LifeWell together

1. [Sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Find the TickTick lists that are really about family — a parent's medicines, a child's appointments — and add those people in [People](/domains/people).
3. Move a family list's dated items to the LifeWell [calendar](/features/calendar-and-appointments). The month view shows them with everything else dated in your record.
4. If a list was someone's medicines, put them in [health](/domains/health) instead, with the dose, the schedule and a [reminder](/features/reminders).
5. Habits and tasks stay in TickTick.

Recordings made with Speak it stay private until you delete them.
