# Moving from TickTick to LifeWell

**A task and habit app with calendar views, free with a yearly Premium.** Choose TickTick if you want tasks, habits and a calendar in one app on every platform. LifeWell has no task list yet. Choose LifeWell if the calendar is for the people in it, and you want their memories, tree and health records in the same record.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [LifeWell and TickTick](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/ticktick).

## How to move across

1. [Open LifeWell and sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Add the people you track in TickTick ([people](/domains/people)), starting with the ones you see most.
3. Keep dates with them ([calendar and appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments)) and set [reminders](/features/reminders).
4. Leave the rest in TickTick until you know what you want to move. LifeWell has no import from it.

## How they differ

| Feature | LifeWell | TickTick |
|---|---|---|
| A record for each person in your life | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Draws your family tree | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Memories with a photo, a date and a place | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Speak it, and it is written down | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://ticktick.com/about/upgrade" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Share a page with someone who has no account | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Appointments with a reminder | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://ticktick.com/about/upgrade" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Medications, conditions and results kept | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Notes | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://ticktick.com/about/upgrade" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Download your own data | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Android app | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://ticktick.com/about/upgrade" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://ticktick.com/about/upgrade" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| One plan for a household | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |

LifeWell keeps each Speak it recording privately for you, and you can delete it at any time. It cannot print your tree for a wall, it has no to-do list for today, and it has no iPhone app. It is not medical advice.
