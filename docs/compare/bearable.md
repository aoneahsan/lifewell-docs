# Moving from Bearable to LifeWell

Bearable shows how symptoms, mood, sleep and medication move together over months, for people with long-term conditions. Most of it is free, and you can export and delete everything from inside the app.

LifeWell keeps the health record next to the rest of life: the people, the memories and the appointments.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Bearable and LifeWell on one page: [LifeWell and Bearable](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/bearable).

## Leaving Bearable with your history

1. Export your data from inside Bearable before anything else.
2. [Sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
3. Mood and sleep are trackers in [health](/domains/health), where the latest entry of the day wins. Medicines go on the medications page.
4. Set up each condition as a standing record, and log every episode under it.
5. Put the appointments themselves on the [calendar](/features/calendar-and-appointments), with a checklist of what to raise.
6. Keep Bearable for the reports you take to appointments. LifeWell is not medical advice.

## Bearable and LifeWell

| Feature | LifeWell | Bearable |
|---|---|---|
| Medications, conditions and results kept | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://bearable.app/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Download your own data | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://bearable.app/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://bearable.app/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

Bearable's pages did not state a record per person, a family tree, memories, speaking an entry, share links, appointments, notes, an Android app or a household plan.
