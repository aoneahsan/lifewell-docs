# Moving from Bearable to LifeWell

**A symptom and mood tracker for people with long-term conditions, mostly free, with export and delete in the app.** Choose Bearable if you want to see how symptoms, mood, sleep and medication move together over months, with reports for your appointments. Choose LifeWell if you want the health record and the rest of life, the people, memories and appointments, in one place.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [LifeWell and Bearable](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/bearable).

## How to move across

1. [Open LifeWell and sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Add the medicines, conditions and results you want to keep ([health](/domains/health)).
3. Add appointments with a reminder ([calendar and appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments)).
4. Keep Bearable if your clinic or device depends on it. LifeWell keeps your own record; it is not medical advice and does not connect to your doctor's system.

## How they differ

| Feature | LifeWell | Bearable |
|---|---|---|
| A record for each person in your life | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Draws your family tree | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Memories with a photo, a date and a place | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Speak it, and it is written down | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Share a page with someone who has no account | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Appointments with a reminder | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Medications, conditions and results kept | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://bearable.app/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Notes | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Download your own data | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://bearable.app/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Android app | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://bearable.app/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| One plan for a household | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |

LifeWell does not keep the audio of a conversation yet: it writes down what you say. It cannot print your tree for a wall, it has no to-do list for today, and it has no iPhone app. It is not medical advice.
