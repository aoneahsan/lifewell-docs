# Moving from Elfie to LifeWell

Elfie is a free app for vitals and medication that pays coins for each healthy action. If rewards keep you taking readings, that is a fair reason to use it.

LifeWell has no points, no coins and no streaks. It is a quiet record of health, kept with the people, memories and appointments of your life.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The app's own comparison: [LifeWell and Elfie](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/elfie).

## Moving your readings

1. [Sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Blood pressure is a tracker in [health](/domains/health). Enter the readings you want to keep — a second reading on the same day replaces the first.
3. Add your medicines with their schedule. Medication [reminders](/features/reminders) are on by default.
4. Looking after a parent? An appointment records who it is with, so their check-up is clear on your [calendar](/features/calendar-and-appointments).
5. Keep Elfie if the coins are what keep you going. LifeWell is not medical advice.
