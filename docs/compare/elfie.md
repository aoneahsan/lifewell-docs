# Moving from Elfie to LifeWell

**A free app for tracking vitals and medication that pays coins for each healthy action.** Choose Elfie if rewards and coins help you keep up with blood pressure readings and medication. Choose LifeWell if you would rather have no points at all, and a quiet record of health kept with the people, memories and appointments of your life.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [LifeWell and Elfie](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/elfie).

## How to move across

1. [Open LifeWell and sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Add the medicines, conditions and results you want to keep ([health](/domains/health)).
3. Add appointments with a reminder ([calendar and appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments)).
4. Keep Elfie if your clinic or device depends on it. LifeWell keeps your own record; it is not medical advice and does not connect to your doctor's system.

## How they differ

| Feature | LifeWell | Elfie |
|---|---|---|
| A record for each person in your life | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Draws your family tree | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Memories with a photo, a date and a place | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Speak it, and it is written down | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Share a page with someone who has no account | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Appointments with a reminder | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Medications, conditions and results kept | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://www.elfie.co/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Notes | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Download your own data | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Android app | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://www.elfie.co/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| One plan for a household | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://www.elfie.co/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

LifeWell does not keep the audio of a conversation yet: it writes down what you say. It cannot print your tree for a wall, it has no to-do list for today, and it has no iPhone app. It is not medical advice.
