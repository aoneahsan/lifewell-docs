# Moving from Evergreen Life to LifeWell

Evergreen Life is a free UK app for people registered with a GP in England. It pulls in your GP record, books GP appointments and orders NHS repeat prescriptions.

LifeWell suits someone outside England, or anyone who wants a record they write themselves for every person they care for, with memories and the tree beside it.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Both, row by row: [LifeWell and Evergreen Life](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/evergreen-life).

## Using it alongside your GP record

1. Download your GP medical record from Evergreen Life and keep the file.
2. [Sign in to LifeWell](/getting-started/first-run).
3. Copy the parts you want in your own record — conditions, medications, vaccinations — into [health](/domains/health).
4. Add the people you care for in [People](/domains/people), and their appointments to the [calendar](/features/calendar-and-appointments) with a [reminder](/features/reminders).
5. Keep booking GP appointments and ordering prescriptions in Evergreen Life. LifeWell does not connect to the NHS or your GP, and it is not medical advice.
