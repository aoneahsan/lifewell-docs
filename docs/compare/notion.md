# Moving from Notion to LifeWell

**A general workspace of pages and databases you can shape into a life organiser yourself.** Choose Notion if you enjoy building your own system of pages and databases and want it to double as a work tool. Choose LifeWell if you want people, memories, a tree, health and appointments already shaped and connected, with nothing to build.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [LifeWell and Notion](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/notion).

## How to move across

1. [Open LifeWell and sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Add the people you track in Notion ([people](/domains/people)), starting with the ones you see most.
3. Keep dates with them ([calendar and appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments)) and set [reminders](/features/reminders).
4. Leave the rest in Notion until you know what you want to move. LifeWell has no import from it.

## How they differ

| Feature | LifeWell | Notion |
|---|---|---|
| A record for each person in your life | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Draws your family tree | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Memories with a photo, a date and a place | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Speak it, and it is written down | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Share a page with someone who has no account | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Appointments with a reminder | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://www.notion.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Medications, conditions and results kept | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Notes | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://www.notion.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Download your own data | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://www.notion.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Android app | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://www.notion.com/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| One plan for a household | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |

LifeWell does not keep the audio of a conversation yet: it writes down what you say. It cannot print your tree for a wall, it has no to-do list for today, and it has no iPhone app. It is not medical advice.
