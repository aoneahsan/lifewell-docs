# Moving from Notion to LifeWell

Notion is a workspace you build yourself — pages, databases, a calendar — and the same tool you use for work. Some people enjoy building a life organiser in it.

LifeWell is already built. People, memories, a tree, health and appointments are shaped and linked to each other, with nothing to design.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Notion beside LifeWell, on the app: [LifeWell and Notion](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/notion).

## Out of a Notion database

1. Export the workspace from Notion as CSV. It also offers HTML, Markdown and PDF.
2. [Sign in to LifeWell](/getting-started/first-run) and open the import page in [People](/domains/people). Choose the CSV from your people database — the first column is read as the name, so put the names there.
3. Fill in each person, and link family to each other so the [tree](/domains/people) draws.
4. Dated rows become [appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments), with a [reminder](/features/reminders) where you want one. Free text becomes [notes](/domains/everyday), which are rich text in folders.
5. Keep Notion for work. LifeWell has no pages or databases to shape.
