# Moving from CareClinic to LifeWell

**A symptom, medication and health-records tracker with reports to share with a doctor or carer.** Choose CareClinic if you manage a chronic condition and want validated assessments, care plans and a 30-day report for your provider. Choose LifeWell if you want a plain record of medications, results and appointments on a free plan, kept with the people in your life.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [LifeWell and CareClinic](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/careclinic).

## How to move across

1. [Open LifeWell and sign in](/getting-started/first-run).
2. Add the medicines, conditions and results you want to keep ([health](/domains/health)).
3. Add appointments with a reminder ([calendar and appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments)).
4. Keep CareClinic if your clinic or device depends on it. LifeWell keeps your own record; it is not medical advice and does not connect to your doctor's system.

## How they differ

| Feature | LifeWell | CareClinic |
|---|---|---|
| A record for each person in your life | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Draws your family tree | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Memories with a photo, a date and a place | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Speak it, and it is written down | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Share a page with someone who has no account | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://careclinic.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Appointments with a reminder | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://careclinic.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Medications, conditions and results kept | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://careclinic.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Notes | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| Download your own data | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://careclinic.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| Android app | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://careclinic.io/pricing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| A free plan that stays free | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| One plan for a household | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |

LifeWell does not keep the audio of a conversation yet: it writes down what you say. It cannot print your tree for a wall, it has no to-do list for today, and it has no iPhone app. It is not medical advice.

Check it yourself: <a href="https://careclinic.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">CareClinic website</a>.
