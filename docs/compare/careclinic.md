# Moving from CareClinic to LifeWell

CareClinic is for managing a chronic condition: symptoms, medications, labs, vaccines and imaging in one searchable place, with validated assessments, care plans and a 30-day report for your provider.

LifeWell keeps a plainer record of medications, results and appointments on a free plan, alongside the people in your life.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The side-by-side is on [LifeWell and CareClinic](https://lifewell.aoneahsan.com/compare/careclinic).

## Moving records out of CareClinic

1. Make a 30-day report in CareClinic to copy from.
2. [Sign in to LifeWell](/getting-started/first-run).
3. Enter lab results and vaccinations on their own pages in [health](/domains/health). A condition is a standing record, and each flare-up is an episode under it.
4. Appointment summaries become [appointments](/features/calendar-and-appointments) with a checklist, so each answer stays beside its question.
5. When a clinician wants to see it, LifeWell's PDF export is made for that ([export](/your-data/export)). It leaves some things out on purpose.
6. Keep CareClinic if your care plan lives there. LifeWell is not medical advice.
