# About this site

This is the official public documentation for **LifeWell** — a private health and wellness companion that runs on the web, on Android, and as a Chrome-Web-Store-compliant browser extension.

## What's documented here

Every user-facing feature of LifeWell — from blood pressure tracking to family-tree management to the kegel trainer timer — is documented in dedicated pages, organized by feature domain. The structure follows the [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) framework, mixing tutorials (for newcomers), how-to guides (for specific tasks), reference (exhaustive option detail), and explanation (architecture and design rationale).

If you can't find what you're looking for, [file a documentation issue on GitHub](https://github.com/aoneahsan/lifewell-docs/issues) or email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com).
